Privacy Policy

ScamShield Security Assistant

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

ScamShield ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use the ScamShield extension.

1. Information We Collect

ScamShield is built with privacy-first principles. We only process data required to perform real-time security scanning and phishing analysis:

  • Webpage Content & Metadata: When you scan an active tab, ScamShield extracts page metadata (title, URL, description, and links) to evaluate scam indicators.
  • No Personal Identification (PII): We do NOT collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment info, or browsing history.
  • No Keystroke / Form Logging: We do NOT track keyboard inputs, form data, or background web activity.

2. How We Use Information

Data processed by ScamShield is strictly used to:

  • Detect phishing, credential harvesting attempts, and malicious links.
  • Identify webpage tasks to recommend safer alternatives when a site is unsafe.
  • Perform real-time threat analysis using local taxonomy rules and AI engines.

3. Data Processing & Third Parties

Security rules are processed locally or via our backend proxy. Anonymized webpage metadata may be processed by AI model endpoints (e.g. Google Gemini API) to suggest task-matched safer alternatives. We NEVER sell or monetize user data.

4. Permissions Required

  • activeTab: Accesses the current tab URL and content only during security scans.
  • scripting: Extracts text and links from the scanned webpage for threat analysis.
  • storage: Stores user preferences locally on your device.
